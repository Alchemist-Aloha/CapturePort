# Website
Mode: Persuade. Audience: Linux photographers. Action: explore and download.
Proof: five existing application screenshots. Pinned direction: Apple-inspired.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A shoot's safe landing, demonstrated through the real app rather than invented marketing UI.
OWN-WORLD: Near-white, forest-green actions, Outfit display, Spectral wordmark, large native screenshots.
STORY: Understand the importer, explore its steps, see how it protects files, download Linux packages.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim navigation, centered oversized two-line headline and download action, followed by a full-width app screenshot.
FORM: User-pinned Apple-style product showcase; no randomized aesthetic replaces the explicit brief. Signature interaction is the keyboard-accessible five-screen walkthrough.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
